Measurement guide

  1. 01
    Use steady front light

    Face a window or lamp. Avoid a bright window behind you and flickering LEDs.

  2. 02
    Fill the frame

    Keep your face roughly an arm’s length away, looking mostly straight at the camera.

  3. 03
    Stay still for 12 seconds

    Rest the device on something solid. Talking and head movement can overwhelm the pulse signal.

What this number means

This is an experimental remote-photoplethysmography estimate, not a medical measurement. Ordinary webcams compress colour, adjust exposure, and run at uneven frame rates; movement and lighting can still produce a plausible but incorrect result. Use a validated device for health decisions. If you feel unwell, seek medical help rather than relying on this page.
